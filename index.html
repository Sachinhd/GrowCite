Common questions

Quick answers. For anything else, message us.

Is it free for job seekers?

Yes. We never charge candidates for registration, interviews or placement.

How fast can you fill a role?

Most employers receive a first shortlist within 48 hours. Timelines for senior or niche roles can be longer.

How are your fees charged to employers?

Fees depend on the role and hiring type (permanent, contract or bulk). Message us the requirement and we will share a clear quote.

Which cities do you cover?

We hire across India, with strong networks in Pune, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR and Hyderabad. Remote roles are also available.

How do I send my CV?

Message us on WhatsApp or email it to us. Mention your preferred role, location and experience.
